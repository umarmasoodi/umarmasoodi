## How I think about marketing

- **Revenue first.** Marketing must grow the business. If it does not bring customers or sales, it is not working.
- **Brand is an asset.** A strong brand lowers the cost of every sale. I build brands for the long term, not just the next campaign.
- **Spend smart, not big.** A clear strategy beats a big budget. I have helped brands win against much bigger competitors.
- **Decisions come from data.** I measure what matters, and I cut what does not work.
